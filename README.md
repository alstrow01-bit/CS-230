## The Gaming Room Software Design

### Briefly summarize The Gaming Room client and their software requirements. Who was the client? What type of software did they want you to design?

The Gaming Room was a client that wanted to expand their game called Draw It or Lose It. The game was originally made for Android, but they wanted it to work on other platforms too. The game involves teams guessing images as they are drawn. They needed a system that could support multiple users, manage games and teams, and work across different devices.

### What did you do particularly well in developing this documentation?

I think I did a good job explaining the different operating platforms and comparing their advantages and disadvantages. I was also able to explain why certain platforms would work better for the game. I tried to keep the document organized so it would be easy to understand.

### What about the process of working through a design document did you find helpful when developing the code?

Working through the design document helped me understand how the application should work before starting the coding process. It helped me plan out the different parts of the system and understand how they would communicate with each other. Having a plan made it easier to understand what needed to be done.

### If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?

If I could revise one part of my work, I would improve the section about security. I would add more details about protecting user information and preventing unauthorized access. I think this would make the design stronger and help explain how the game could stay secure.

### How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your software design? Why is it so important to consider the user's needs when designing?

I focused on what The Gaming Room wanted, which was making their game available on different platforms while keeping it easy to use. I considered things like performance, security, and how multiple players would connect to the game. Understanding the user's needs is important because the software should solve their problems and work the way they expect.

### How did you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a similar software application?

I approached the design by reviewing the requirements first and then comparing different platforms and system architectures. I looked at the strengths and weaknesses of each option before making a recommendation. In the future, I would use the same approach by planning everything out, using diagrams when needed, and testing different parts of the application to make sure they work correctly.
